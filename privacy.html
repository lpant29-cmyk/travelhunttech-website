Privacy Policy for Comment AutoReply Buddy

Last updated: July 2026

Comment AutoReply Buddy ("the App") is a tool that automatically responds to comments on connected Instagram accounts using AI-generated replies. The App is developed and operated by Travel Hunt Group.

Information We Access

The App accesses comment text and basic profile information (such as username) on Instagram posts belonging to accounts that have authorized the App. The App does not access private messages, personal contact details, or any data beyond what Instagram's API provides for connected accounts.

How We Use Information

Comment text is sent to a third-party AI service (Anthropic's Claude API) solely to generate a relevant reply. Comment data is not stored permanently, sold, or shared with any other third party, and is not used for advertising purposes.

Data Retention

The App does not maintain a permanent database of comments or replies beyond what is needed to operate the auto-reply feature in real time.

Third-Party Services

The App uses Meta's Instagram Graph API to receive and respond to comments, and Anthropic's Claude API to generate reply text. Each of these providers has its own privacy policy governing their handling of data.

Contact
