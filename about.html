About Travel Hunt Group

Travel Hunt Group started with a simple focus: helping travel businesses manage their technology. Over time, our work expanded naturally into online marketing, sales support and business development, and today we act as a full technology and growth partner for the travel companies we work with, while keeping the same name and the same hands-on approach we started with.

We are based in Haridwar, India, and currently support clients in the United States, helping them manage the day-to-day technical and marketing work behind their travel businesses so they can focus on serving their own customers.

We take a close, partnership-based approach with every client, treating their goals as our own and staying closely involved in the details of their technology, marketing and growth.
