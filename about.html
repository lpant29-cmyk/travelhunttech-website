About Travel Hunt Group

Travel Hunt Group started with a simple focus: helping travel businesses manage their technology, marketing and growth. That work is still at the core of what we do, supporting clients in the United States with the day-to-day technical and marketing engine behind their travel business.

Over time, that experience became the foundation for something bigger. Alongside client work, we've started building and running our own products, beginning with Comment AutoReply Buddy, an Instagram tool that automatically replies to comments in real time. It's the first of what we expect to be a growing group of ventures.

Travel Hunt Group is based in Haridwar, India. Whether we're supporting a client's travel business or building a product of our own, our approach stays the same: stay close to the details, move quickly, and focus on real results.
